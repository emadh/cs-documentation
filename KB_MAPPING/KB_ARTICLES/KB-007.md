## Content requirements

Concepts:
- A tile’s own quantity and its children’s quantity are different.
- A ghosted tile has stock only in children.
- A 3D parent can look full because of children and still not be draggable.
- Read-only users see the same canvas with a shorter hover menu.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in. Read-only users are sent to the read-only map.

Customer actions:
1. Click Map.
2. Read a tile: a number is its own quantity, a number in parentheses is in children.
3. Try to drag only a tile whose own quantity is above 0.

Options and settings: None.

Expected results: Display only.

Errors and troubleshooting: None.

Limitations:
- Do not say a ghosted tile can be dragged.
- Do not claim read-only users cannot drag. The move handle is still drawn and drag was not separately blocked.

Example: A room shows 0 (12) because the shelves inside hold the stock.
