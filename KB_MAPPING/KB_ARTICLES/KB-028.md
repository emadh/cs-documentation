## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Both locations must be real and different.
- Destination becomes its current quantity plus what you moved.
- Source becomes what you left behind on the Transfer page.
- A map drag of quantity 1 sets that product’s source quantity to 0. That is not move-1-and-keep-the-rest.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. Drop a location or a product row onto a different location.
2. If the one-unit comment appears, Cancel to stop, or save knowing the source for that product becomes 0.
3. Otherwise set partial quantities and click Transfer.
4. On Start, pick source then destination. The same place is rejected.

Options and settings: Transfer Everything moves all remaining, like check-out everything.

Expected results: One transfer activity.

Errors and troubleshooting: “The Source can't be the Destination!”

Limitations:
- If loading the destination product throws, destination quantity is treated as 0 and then set to the moved amount only. Mention this as a failure case, not the normal add.

Example: Move 4 from Shelf A to Shelf B and leave the rest on A. Separately, drag a single unit and expect the source to show 0.
