## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Product numbers cannot be renamed.
- AUTO numbers are CSN- plus nine digits.
- Create skips existing numbers. It does not update them.
- Duplicate numbers in one import file are rejected.
- Clone can keep Auto on to avoid a clash.
- Delete is the catalog delete, not a one-location quantity delete.
- The old dialogs are not the live path.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only, for changes.

Customer actions:
1. To change an identity, clone to a new number and delete the old one if you mean to retire it.
2. To fix an import, remove duplicate numbers from the file.
3. After delete, do not expect the old editor to keep working. A missing product shows Product Error.

Options and settings: Clone Auto on by default.

Expected results: A new number, or the old product removed.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Product number already exists”. “That product number already exists!” “No Poduct Number!” when a manual number is blank.

Limitations:
- Do not promise that delete only removes one location’s quantity.
- Do not describe what productDeleter removes beyond the warning and the expiration call.

Example: Clone SKU-1 to SKU-2, then delete SKU-1.
