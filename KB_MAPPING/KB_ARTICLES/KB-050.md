## Content requirements

Concepts:
- All Locations and one location show different rows.
- Zero-quantity products can be hidden.
- Map search with no API match falls back to titles.
- A location can look empty while its children have stock.
- Read-only users see a different map and product page, not a missing account.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in.

Customer actions:
1. Clear the product search.
2. Switch between All Locations and the location you expect.
3. Turn on zero-quantity products if the row might be zero.
4. On the map, clear Search Map.
5. If you are read-only, confirm you are on the read-only pages.

Options and settings: Product images off hides photos, not rows.

Expected results: The item is visible or the empty-state sentence explains why.

Errors and troubleshooting: “No matches found!” “No products yet”. “Location is empty...” “This location is empty but its sub-locations are not”.

Limitations:
- Do not claim the header search and the product-list search match the same fields.

Example: A zero-quantity SKU reappears after the display setting is turned on.
