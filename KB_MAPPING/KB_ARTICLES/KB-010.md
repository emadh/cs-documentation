## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Peek shows up to 25 products.
- View opens the product list filtered to that location.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in.

Customer actions:
1. Click a location.
2. Read the rows. Escape closes the peek.
3. Use View, or View Products in the editor, to open Products for that location.

Options and settings: While map search is active, the peek includes the search terms.

Expected results: No stock change from viewing.

Errors and troubleshooting: None.

Limitations:
- A specific location’s product list shows quantity greater than 0 there. If the location is empty but children are not, the list says so.

Example: Click a truck tile, see three products, then open the full list.
