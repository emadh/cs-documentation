## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The map is locked by default.
- Dragging moves the tile, not the inventory.
- Reparent keeps nested locations with the one you move.
- Tile size follows children. There is no resize handle.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. Desktop for multi-select.

Customer actions:
1. If a drop snaps back, unlock the map.
2. Drag the diamond header to move a primary location.
3. Use the clasp, click a new parent or empty canvas, and confirm Move location?
4. Hold Ctrl and click several tiles, or Ctrl-drag a rectangle, then drag the group.

Options and settings: Empty canvas makes the location primary. ABC sort is display order only and is covered in Navigating the Map.

Expected results: Coordinates change for this user. Inventory stays on the same location ids.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Could not move location”. Lock on cancels an in-progress reparent.

Limitations:
- Fewer than two children skips ABC sort. Overlap is rejected while locked.

Example: Drag Receiving to the left. Move Bin 3 from Shelf A onto Shelf B.
