## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Vertical needs at least two children.
- A child that has its own children must already be 3D.
- 3D hides children in an overlay. These are layout only.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. A parent with children.

Customer actions:
1. Hover More actions, then V, or use Vertical on a narrow screen.
2. If the tooltip says to turn a child to 3D first, do that, then try Vertical again.
3. Click 3D on a parent. Click the parent to open the stack. Click Add inside it to create a child.

Options and settings: None beyond the two modes.

Expected results: This user’s layout. Inventory unchanged.

Errors and troubleshooting: Tooltips include “Needs at least two children”, “Turn off 3D first”, and “A child has its own locations. Turn that child to 3D first”.

Limitations:
- Vertical is hidden inside a 3D stack.
- Do not call the sloped tile edge a 3D label.

Example: Turn a closet with three shelves into a vertical stack.
