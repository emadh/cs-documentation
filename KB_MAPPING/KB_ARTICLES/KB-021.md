## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Header Enter stores the term and opens Products.
- Comma-separated terms are combined with AND.
- Each term matches product number, name, and every custom field, including hidden columns. Not price or quantity.
- Search forces All Locations and hides the pager.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in.

Customer actions:
1. Type in the header and press Enter.
2. Read the chips.
3. Remove a chip to widen the search.

Options and settings: No AND/OR syntax beyond commas. % and _ act as wildcards.

Expected results: A filtered catalog view. No data change.

Errors and troubleshooting: “No matches found!”

Limitations:
- Do not say search matches buy price, sell price, or quantity.

Example: Search “gloves, large” to require both words.
