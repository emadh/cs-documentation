## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Forgot-password reset has no minimum length. The link host is www.cyberstockroom.com.
- Settings password change requires 6 characters and is hidden for Google-linked users.
- Linking Google does not change the CyberStockroom email.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Public for login. Signed in for password change and linking.

Customer actions:
1. Enter email and password, or click Google.
2. Use forgot password, open the email, and set a new password.
3. In Settings, click Update Your Password, enter old, new, and repeat.
4. If Google is not linked, use the link button.

Options and settings: Login placeholders me@email.com and a masked password.

Expected results: A session. Password change returns to Settings.

Errors and troubleshooting: “The email or password is not correct...” Password change: incorrect, don’t match, too short, or must be different. Quote those sentences.

Limitations:
- Do not invent a Google error sentence for a normal login. It was not fully traced.
- Invitation acceptance is the team article.

Example: Reset a forgotten password from the login page.
