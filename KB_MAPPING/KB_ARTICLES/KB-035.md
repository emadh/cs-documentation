## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Check In and Check Out are not pure type filters. They keep adjusts that moved the matching way, and transfers only when one end is inside the location filter.
- With no location selected, transfers are removed from both.
- Users match the stored first name, not the user id.
- Keyword matches comment, product number, product name, and every custom field.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in. Read-only people are omitted from the user chips. Their old rows can still appear.

Customer actions:
1. Choose a type.
2. Choose a date preset or a range.
3. Choose a person and a location.
4. Type a keyword. There is no AND or OR syntax.

Options and settings: Date presets: All Time, Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, This Month. All Time starts at 1 Jan 2011. The picker max is today.

Expected results: A filtered list. Changing filters does not reset the start index.

Errors and troubleshooting: A bad date falls back to the last 90 days.

Limitations:
- Two people with the same first name cannot be separated. A renamed person no longer matches old rows.
- % and _ stay wildcards.
- Location includes descendants.

Example: Check In, Last 7 Days, one room, keyword gloves.
