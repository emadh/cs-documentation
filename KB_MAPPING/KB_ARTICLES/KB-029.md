## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Mode is derived. Source only checks out. Destination only checks in. Both transfer.
- Save posts to the inventory API. What that API does to stock is Uncertain.
- Quick Scan does not create a product.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. Open Quick Scan.
2. Scan a location, then products, or scan products after a kiosk has locked the location.
3. Save.

Options and settings: Placeholder “ready to scan...”. Kiosk modes are the next article.

Expected results: “{N} Transactions completed successfully.” or, on mobile, “Transaction saved”.

Errors and troubleshooting: “You have to select at least one location and one product!” “There was a problem saving the transaction!”

Limitations:
- Do not state the resulting on-hand numbers. Verify them live or omit them.
- No lot, expiration, or kit fields.

Example: Scan a bin, scan three items, save a check-out.
