## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Self-serve buttons appear only when the payment method contains Stripe.
- Online Invoice does not get those buttons.
- Do not publish dollar amounts. They are images.
- The enterprise image has no click.
- Unpaid trial is 14 days. After that, other pages redirect to Settings.
- The trial, upgrade, and payment banners stay hidden. Do not tell users to look for them.
- There is no documented plan-capacity limit.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Admin for cancel and billing history. Update Account blocks read-only only, not non-admin.

Customer actions:
1. Open Subscription and read the summary.
2. If the buttons are there, use Update Plan, Change Card, or Cancel.
3. Cancel asks “Are you sure you want to cancel your subscription?” Choose Cancel Subscription or Keep Subscription.
4. Open Billing History for the charges table.

Options and settings: Monthly and annual images. Live price ids may be named only if you need them internally. Do not print prices.

Expected results: A plan or card change, or a cancel that sets confirmed payment back to 0 and applies the trial rule again.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Please contact admin@cyberstockroom.com to change your subscription or payment method”. Billing exception: “No billing history to show...” Empty plan: “No Active Subscription”.

Limitations:
- PayPal and Stripe-invoice customers do not get the self-serve buttons.
- An invoice table is built and not shown. Do not describe it.
- People sent to Settings are not automatically shown the plan popup.

Example: Update a card. Read the next payment date. A lapsed trial can open Settings and not the map.
