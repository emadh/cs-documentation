## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Readable Format is on by default.
- The download ignores the 15-row cap.
- The last four headers, old quantity, new quantity, difference, and cost difference, are never filled.
- Commas in some fields become underscores.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, including read-only.

Customer actions:
1. Set the filters first.
2. Click the CSV download.
3. Leave Readable Format checked unless you want the uniform file described in the tooltip.

Options and settings: Tooltip: “If you need to manipulate your data more easily you can uncheck this box for a more uniform file.”

Expected results: A CSV of the filtered history.

Errors and troubleshooting: None specific beyond an empty filter result.

Limitations:
- Do not tell readers the difference columns contain numbers.
- Hover shows at most 11 product lines. Deleted products show [DELETED].

Example: Export last 30 days of check-outs.
