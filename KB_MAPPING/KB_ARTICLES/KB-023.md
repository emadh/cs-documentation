## Content requirements

Concepts:
- This is not a Transfer mode.
- The list editor works only on the editable page, at a real location, on an in-stock cell, and not during search.
- Down stops at 0.
- The on-screen number updates when the request is sent.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. A specific location.

Customer actions:
1. On the product map, click a location, type a whole number, and press Enter.
2. Or on the product list, edit the quantity cell and confirm.
3. Read the failure alert if it appears. The number may already have changed on screen.

Options and settings: Arrow keys step the value on a fine pointer.

Expected results: An adjust request with source and destination equal to that location.

Errors and troubleshooting: “There was a problem updating the quantity in this location...”

Limitations:
- Do not document an Adjust button on Transfer.
- Invalid map input becomes 0.
- Timeout is 3 seconds.

Example: Change a bin from 4 to 6 without checking out and back in.
