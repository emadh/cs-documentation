## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The map is the layout of locations. Products are the catalog. Quantities live at locations. All Locations is not a physical place.
- Check-in, check-out, and transfer are different actions.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- A signed-in user. Read-only users can look, not create.

Customer actions:
1. Sign in.
2. Open Map and notice locations as tiles.
3. Open Products and notice the catalog, including All Locations.
4. Open the activity icon and notice that history is a record, not an undo button.

Options and settings: None on this overview.

Expected results: No data changes.

Errors and troubleshooting: None.

Limitations:
- Do not describe Welcome or getting-started as a wizard. Both redirect to the map.
- Do not describe a plan-capacity limit.

Example: A storeroom uses the map for rooms and shelves, the product list for item names, and check-in to place quantities on a shelf.
