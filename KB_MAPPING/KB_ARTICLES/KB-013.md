## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Structure clone and stock copy are different.
- Ctrl+drop warns that existing items at the destination are cleared first.
- Auto-rename of sub-locations is off by default.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. Ctrl+drop needs a location with its own quantity above 0, and multi-select must be off.

Customer actions:
1. Open clone, type a name up to 255 characters. The suggestion is the name plus (Copy).
2. Leave Auto-Rename Sub-Locations off unless a word or hyphen part should change in the children.
3. Confirm.
4. Separately, hold Ctrl, drag a full location onto another, and confirm the clear-first message.

Options and settings: Copy names run up to (Copy 999). Same name as the source or a sibling is blocked.

Expected results: Structure clone creates locations. Whether it copies quantities is Uncertain. Ctrl+drop is a stock copy.

Errors and troubleshooting: Could not complete the clone if the API fails. Do not invent the error text.

Limitations:
- Do not say the structure clone copies quantities.
- Blocked during multi-select and when the drag is a product chip.

Example: Clone Aisle A to Aisle B and rename A- bins to B-. Then Ctrl-drop one bin’s stock onto an empty bin.
