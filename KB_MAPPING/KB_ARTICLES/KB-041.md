## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The unmarked invite role is Admin.
- An email that already belongs to this account does not change role.
- There is no last-admin protection.
- Accepting an invite does not show the role.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Admin for invite, role change, and delete. Admin and User can see the list. Read-only cannot.

Customer actions:
1. Open Profile & Team.
2. Search if there are more than 12 people.
3. Invite with an email and a role.
4. The person opens the link and creates a password.
5. Change a role from another person’s card, or DELETE them.

Options and settings: Roles ADMIN, USER, and READ-ONLY. Page size 12.

Expected results: A new user, a role change, or a deleted user. The invite copies the inviter’s expiration flag and map coordinates without the share marker.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Please enter an email address.” “Please enter a valid email address.” “Unable to Invite - Try Again?” “Please create a password to accept your invitation.” Delete confirm names the user.

Limitations:
- A User sees Invite and gets the unable-to-invite error. Say the action is for admins.

Example: Invite a read-only auditor. Later change them to User.
