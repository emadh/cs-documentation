## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The catalog rows already exist.
- This page adds stock. It does not create the products.
- Skip leaves them with no placed stock.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- A batch of just-created products. Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. When the check-in page opens, click a location. The suggested one is the default check-in location.
2. Enter whole-number quantities. At least one line must be 1 or more.
3. Save, or add a comment.
4. Or click Skip.

Options and settings: If every imported line names a location, the map is hidden and each line uses its own location.

Expected results: An additive check-in. Returning to an open transfer does not change stock until that transfer is saved.

Errors and troubleshooting: “No products are waiting to be checked in.” “Each quantity must be a whole number, 0 or greater.” “Select a location on the map before checking in.” “A location in this import could not be found.”

Limitations:
- One bad imported location aborts the whole save.

Example: Three new products, all checked into Receiving.
