## Content requirements

Concepts:
- All Locations is the catalog view.
- A specific location lists products with quantity above 0 there.
- Zero-quantity rows can be hidden by a per-user setting.
- Cost value is quantity times buy price. It is calculated, not stored.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in.

Customer actions:
1. Open Products on All Locations.
2. Switch to one location.
3. If the location is empty but children are not, read that sentence.
4. Compare a quantity edited on the product map with the number in the list.

Options and settings: Show zero-quantity products is a Settings option.

Expected results: Display. Editing quantity is a different article.

Errors and troubleshooting: Empty catalog: “No products yet”. Empty location: “Location is empty...”

Limitations:
- Search forces All Locations for that request.

Example: All Locations shows 20. The truck shows 3. The rest is in other places.
