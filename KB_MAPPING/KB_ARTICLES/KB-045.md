## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Default locations are per user. Clear means none, stored as All Locations.
- Time zone and currency are account-wide and admin-only.
- Time zone offsets are whole hours from -12 through 13. Two labels share +5. There are no half-hour zones.
- Currency is any non-empty symbol, default $.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Admin or User for locations. Read-only is unauthorized. Admin for time zone and currency.

Customer actions:
1. Open Default Check-In Location, pick a place, Save, or CLEAR Selection.
2. Repeat for check-out.
3. Choose a time zone. It saves on change.
4. Type a currency symbol and press Enter.

Options and settings: Fallback time zone -5. Fallback currency $.

Expected results: Later check-ins and check-outs can start at those locations. Activity timestamps use the time zone. Product headers use the symbol.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Error saving timezone. Please try again.” “Please enter a currency symbol”. “Error saving currency symbol. Please try again.”

Limitations:
- The currency symbol does not change the subscription card.
- Do not offer half-hour zones.

Example: Default check-in to Receiving. Currency €. Time zone the warehouse’s GMT offset.
