## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Source is All Locations.
- Click moves 1. There is no check-in everything.
- Save adds the quantities.
- The Start page also adds, but the comment comes after the write.
- If a URL asks for both check-in and check-out, check-out wins.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. A real destination. Empty locations and All Locations as the destination are refused from the map.

Customer actions:
1. From the map, start check-in on a location.
2. Or open Check In from Start or the phone menu.
3. Search or scan. Click a product to take 1. Type a larger quantity when asked.
4. Click Check In. Skip the comment or save one.

Options and settings: New Products and import show only while the destination selection is empty, unless import is hidden.

Expected results: One check-in activity. Quantities increase at the destination.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Please enter a quantity of at least 1.” “Product Number '{scan}' doesn't exist!” on desktop. A mobile miss can open Add Products.

Limitations:
- Do not describe this as subtracting from a source location. The source is the catalog.

Example: Receive 12 boxes into Receiving.
