## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Enter cycles matches. Escape clears.
- Colors use the threshold and max highlight from Settings.
- Quantity 0 is not colored as a low or high tier.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in. Threshold and max highlight are optional.

Customer actions:
1. Type in Search Map and press Enter.
2. Turn on Quantity Threshold and Max Highlight in Settings if you want color.
3. Search again and read low, mid, and max colors.

Options and settings: Threshold off is 0. Turning it on starts at 1. Max highlight must be above the threshold. Off displays as 10.

Expected results: Highlight only. Inventory unchanged.

Errors and troubleshooting: “No results to download” when a search matches nothing and a report is requested.

Limitations:
- Do not list the API’s match fields. On API failure the page matches location titles.

Example: Search “bin” and cycle with Enter. Set threshold 5 and see low tiles.
