## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Under 250 KB, at most 100 columns, UTF-8, commas.
- The first row is the header.
- At least a product number must be mapped.
- Sample headers are examples.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. From Add Products, upload the file.
2. Drag columns onto Product Number and any other fields.
3. Fix row errors.
4. Continue to check-in.

Options and settings: Unmapped quantity stays 1, prices 0, location All Locations. Expiration imports only for new numbers, and only if expirations are on.

Expected results: New numbers are created. Existing numbers keep their fields. Their quantity is staged for check-in.

Errors and troubleshooting: “This file is too large. Use a CSV smaller than 250 KB.” “You must select at least a Product Number to upload.” Duplicate numbers in the file are rejected. Quote the other file errors from the catalog entry PED-012.

Limitations:
- Presets are not enforced on import.
- Do not say an existing product’s description is updated.

Example: A three-column file of number, name, and quantity.
