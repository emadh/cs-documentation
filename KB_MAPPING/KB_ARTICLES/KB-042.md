## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Read-only users are sent to the read-only map, product list, and product editor.
- They cannot transfer, import, or create.
- They can read activity and download some files.
- User can see team and invite UI. The invite API is admin-only.
- There is no last-admin guard.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in as each role if you are capturing screenshots. Use test users.

Customer actions:
1. Compare the Map menu, Products toolbar, and Settings sections for each role.
2. As read-only, open a product and confirm fields are disabled.
3. As admin, change another user’s role.

Options and settings: Effective roles are Admin, User, and Read-only. A legacy Mobile type is treated as User.

Expected results: Permissions, not a data change, except the role change itself.

Errors and troubleshooting: Read-only direct links to Transfer, import, or the default-location picker go to unauthorized or the read-only page.

Limitations:
- Do not say a read-only user cannot drag the map. That was not proven.
- SUPER_USER sees an empty Settings page. Whether any customer has that role was not checked. Do not document it as a choice.

Example: A read-only user can look up a quantity and cannot check it out.
