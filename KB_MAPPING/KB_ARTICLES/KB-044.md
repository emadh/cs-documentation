## Content requirements

Concepts:
- These are per user except where the catalog says otherwise.
- A failed toggle reverts after about 1.5 seconds.
- Expiring soon uses 7, 30, and 365 for week, month, and year.
- Zero quantity locations stay white. That sentence belongs to the threshold, not the product-list toggle.
- Kitting is not in this list.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in. Admin for Share Map Layout.

Customer actions:
1. Open Settings, Options.
2. Turn product images, zero-quantity products, and expirations on or off.
3. Set expiring soon only after expirations are on.
4. Set quantity threshold, then max highlight above it.
5. Open the map gear for popup details. The field list is per account. Do not invent field names.

Options and settings: Expiring soon default 60 days. Threshold on starts at 1. Max highlight off shows 10.

Expected results: This user’s display. Sharing is covered in the share-layout article.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Enable Expirations before using Expiring Soon.” “Expiring soon window must be a valid duration greater than 0.” “The threshold must be a valid number bigger than 0.” “Max highlight must be greater than the quantity threshold.”

Limitations:
- Do not publish the hidden kitting checkbox.

Example: Highlight locations at or below 5 and at or above 100.
