## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Transfer is not a kiosk mode.
- Location scans are ignored in kiosk mode.
- Narrow screens use the mobile Quick Scan page.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. A saved location.

Customer actions:
1. Open the location editor.
2. Click Checkin Kiosk or Checkout Kiosk.
3. Scan products only.
4. Save.

Options and settings: The link carries mode and locationID.

Expected results: Same save as Quick Scan, with the location fixed.

Errors and troubleshooting: Same as Quick Scan, plus “Kiosk mode is active…” when a location scan is attempted. Quote the catalog sentence from XFR-011.

Limitations:
- Do not offer a transfer kiosk.

Example: A receiving desk uses Checkin Kiosk on the dock location.
