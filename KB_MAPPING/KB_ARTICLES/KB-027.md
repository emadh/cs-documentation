## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Destination is All Locations.
- Click moves 1 up to on-hand. Drag moves the rest.
- Save sets the source to the quantity left behind. It does not subtract on this page.
- Start check-out does subtract and stops at 0.
- Activity stores the quantity that moved.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. Stock at the source.

Customer actions:
1. Drag a full location to Drag inventory here to check out, or open Check Out.
2. Move quantities.
3. Click Check Out.
4. Use Check-Out Everything only when you want the whole location. It hides while a map filter is on.

Options and settings: The add-quantity box still says Transfer. Describe the action as check-out anyway.

Expected results: Source quantity becomes what you did not move.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Product '{term}' was not found at this location.” “Please enter a quantity up to {max}.”

Limitations:
- Do not say Transfer check-out subtracts. Say the source is set to the remaining quantity.

Example: Check out 2 of 10 and leave 8.
