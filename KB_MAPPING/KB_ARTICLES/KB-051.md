## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Transfer check-out sets the source to the remaining quantity.
- Start check-out subtracts and stops at 0.
- A one-unit drag on the map sets that product’s source to 0 even if you thought you moved one of many.
- Check-in on Transfer adds. Check-in on Start also adds.
- Direct quantity edit overwrites the typed number.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- A test quantity you can afford to change, or a written example from the catalog.

Customer actions:
1. Identify which screen you used: Transfer, Start, map one-unit, or the quantity cell.
2. For Transfer, compare the quantity you left in the selection with the source.
3. For a one-unit map drag, expect 0 at the source for that product.
4. For a typed quantity, expect the typed number, not an addition.

Options and settings: None.

Expected results: The reader can name which rule applied.

Errors and troubleshooting: “There was a problem updating the quantity in this location...” The on-screen number may already have changed.

Limitations:
- Do not publish Quick Scan’s resulting quantity. It is Uncertain.
- If the destination product fails to load during a transfer, the destination can be set to the moved amount only.

Example: Drag the only unit of a SKU and find 0 left behind. Move 1 of 5 on the Transfer page and find 4 left behind.
