## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Clone copies fields, image when it can, and expiration if set. It does not copy stock, locations, or attachments.
- Delete says it also checks the product out of all locations.
- Select all before delete deletes every product in the account.
- The old clone popup and the quantity-only delete dialog are not the live path.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. Open a product, click Clone Product, leave Auto on or type a new number, and confirm.
2. Or click Delete this product and confirm.
3. From the list, select rows and delete. Read the cannot-be-undone warning.

Options and settings: Auto number is CSN- plus nine digits.

Expected results: A new catalog row, or the product is removed. What else the deleter removes is Uncertain beyond the expiration call the page makes.

Errors and troubleshooting: “You must enter a product number.” “That product number already exists!”

Limitations:
- Do not say clone copies quantities.
- Do not describe delete as a choice between one location and all locations. That dialog is unwired.

Example: Clone a glove to a new color. Delete a discontinued SKU.
