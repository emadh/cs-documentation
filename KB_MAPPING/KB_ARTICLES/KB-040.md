## Content requirements

Concepts:
- A blank name blocks the app with a required-name popup, except on Settings and printable view.
- Name max is 50 and must be unique on the account.
- Email uniqueness is real, but the same-as-old check compares to the first name, not the current email. Do not document that bug as intended behavior. Do not promise a no-op when re-saving the same email.
- Presets 1 through 15, or an upload under 3 MB.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in.

Customer actions:
1. If asked, enter a name and pick an icon.
2. In Settings, change the name and press Enter, or Escape to cancel.
3. Change the email the same way.
4. Click Apply on a preset or an upload.

Options and settings: Accepted images: png, jpeg, gif, webp. Default preset is 1.

Expected results: Name, email, or picture saved. The badge is display only.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Please enter your name.” “Please use 50 characters or fewer.” “Someone sharing this account is using this name already...” Email title: “That email is invalid or already exists.” Image: “Please choose an image file.” “Please choose an image under 3 MB.”

Limitations:
- The Mobile badge in markup is never shown. A stored Mobile user appears as User.

Example: Replace the default avatar and correct a last name.
