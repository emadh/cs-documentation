## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Fields are text. There is no date, number, or dropdown type.
- Names and presets are account-wide. Order and price visibility are per user.
- Price cards appear in Settings only while personalized sort is on.
- Create has no duplicate-name check.
- Delete warns that data is erased. The full cascade was not read.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Admin for create, rename, delete, and presets.

Customer actions:
1. Open Custom Fields.
2. Turn personalized sort on to show Buy and Sell and the arrows.
3. Create a field.
4. Rename it.
5. Open Presets, enter values, optionally allow other.
6. Delete only after exporting, and only if you accept the warning.

Options and settings: Other (Specify). Allow other. Commas in presets become underscores.

Expected results: A new text field, a renamed label, presets, or a deleted definition.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Unable to save the personalized sort preference right now.” “Unable to save the field visibility right now.” Delete confirm includes “Existing data for this field will be erased!”

Limitations:
- Closing the preset popup does not refresh Settings.
- Do not say duplicate names are blocked.
- Do not say every related row was verified as deleted.

Example: Add Supplier with three presets and Other.
