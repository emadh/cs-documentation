## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Delete always includes sub-locations.
- Delete checks products out of those locations.
- The include-sub-locations checkbox is hidden and unread.
- There is no No button. Closing the popup cancels.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only.

Customer actions:
1. Create from + as in the first-map article.
2. Open the location editor and change the name.
3. Delete and read the confirm: “Are your sure you want to DELETE this location?” and the line that sub-locations will be deleted and products checked out.
4. Click YES, or close the popup to cancel.

Options and settings: All Locations cannot be renamed.

Expected results: Rename keeps the same location id. Delete removes the location and descendants after a check-out activity.

Errors and troubleshooting: Duplicate name: “That location name is already in use!” Unknown id shows Location Error.

Limitations:
- The confirm contains the typo “Are your sure”. Quote it or paraphrase without pretending the product says “you”.
- Do not offer a choice to keep sub-locations.

Example: Rename Shelf A to Shelf B. Delete an empty closet and its bins.
