## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Empty-map coaching appears on the designer map.
- Creating a product does not place stock until check-in is saved.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. An account with no locations or no products.

Customer actions:
1. Open Map.
2. Follow the on-screen prompt to add a location.
3. Open Products and click Click to create if the list is empty.
4. Save the product, then check it into the location or skip.

Options and settings: The two-minute check-in lesson title is “2 min lesson about checking in your products”.

Expected results: A location and, if check-in was saved, stock at that location.

Errors and troubleshooting: Duplicate location name: “That location name is already in use!”

Limitations:
- Which coaching card wins for every mix of zero products and zero locations was not fully separated.
- This article overlaps the dedicated first-map, first-product, and first-check-in articles. Keep this one short and link to them.

Example: A new warehouse adds one room, one item, and checks in a quantity of 1.
