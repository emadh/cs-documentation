## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The product report does not include the current search.
- Every product-report download rewrites location paths in the account before the query. Do not publish that as a feature. Do not claim the download is side-effect free if you are verifying data.
- Map report is hidden until some visible location has quantity above 0.
- Group by Location includes empty locations. Group by Product sums.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in. The map button also requires a visible quantity. Read-only is not separately blocked on the product export page.

Customer actions:
1. On Products, click the download icon. Choose columns. Download.
2. On the map, click Download Quantity Report. Choose Group by Location or Group by Product.

Options and settings: Product report defaults: skip zero quantity, skip sub-locations on a specific location, include description, quantity, breakdown, buy, cost, sell, and custom fields. Image off.

Expected results: A CSV. Cost is quantity times buy price, rounded half away from zero.

Errors and troubleshooting: Map export while signed out is unauthorized. Empty map search: “No results to download”.

Limitations:
- Do not say the product report respects the search chips.

Example: Download All Locations with cost. Download the map grouped by product.
