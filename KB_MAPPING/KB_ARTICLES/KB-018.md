## Content requirements

Concepts:
- The product number cannot be renamed.
- Buy and sell show only when those prices are visible and the account is not a records account.
- Presets offer (none), each preset, the current value if it is outside the list, and Other (Specify).

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. Presets are account-wide. Price visibility is per user.

Customer actions:
1. Click Add, or click a product row.
2. Edit the name. Leave prices blank to keep 0, or enter a number that is not negative.
3. Pick a preset or Other (Specify).
4. Save. Admins can click Edit your fields.

Options and settings: Currency rejects non-numeric and negative values.

Expected results: Activity records “Changed '{label}' to '{value}'.”

Errors and troubleshooting: “The Buy Price is not valid!” “The Sell Price is not valid!” The product-name error string is not used unless a numeric check runs. Do not promise that sentence.

Limitations:
- Read-only editor is view only.
- Account type 1 hides prices.

Example: Set Sell Price to 12.50 and a Supplier preset.
