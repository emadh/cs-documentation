## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Drag moves the tile in this user’s layout.
- Reparent changes the parent id. Nested locations stay with it. Quantities stay on the same location ids.
- Copying stock is Ctrl+drop, a different gesture.
- Deleting a location does check stock out. That is not a move.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- A location with stock.

Customer actions:
1. Note the quantity.
2. Unlock if needed and drag the tile.
3. Confirm the quantity is unchanged.
4. Reparent it and confirm again.
5. If you needed the stock in the other place, use transfer or Ctrl+drop instead.

Options and settings: None.

Expected results: Layout changes. Inventory does not.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Could not move location” on a failed reparent.

Limitations:
- Ctrl+drop clears the destination first. Do not recommend it as a simple move.

Example: Slide a shelf two squares right. The same products remain in that shelf.
