## Content requirements

Concepts:
- Read-only cannot create, import, or transfer.
- The map is locked until it is unlocked.
- An expired unpaid trial or a delinquent Stripe payment sends every page except Settings to Settings.
- Those banners are hidden.
- Invite, custom fields, and API keys are admin or beta gated even if a control is visible.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- The affected user.

Customer actions:
1. Confirm the role badge.
2. If a location snaps back, unlock the map.
3. If every page opens Settings, read the subscription section. Do not look for a hidden banner.
4. If import is missing, confirm you are not read-only and the transfer destination is not already chosen.

Options and settings: None.

Expected results: The reader knows which gate applies.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Unable to Invite - Try Again?” Unauthorized pages for read-only. “Could not move location”.

Limitations:
- Do not say the last admin cannot be removed or demoted.
- Do not say a capacity limit caused the redirect.

Example: A read-only user cannot see Add. A locked map refuses an overlapping drop.
