## Content requirements

Concepts:
- AUTO product numbers look like CSN- plus nine digits.
- An empty description becomes the product number.
- Existing product numbers are skipped, not updated.

Prerequisites and permissions:
- Signed in, not read-only. Account type 1 hides prices.

Customer actions:
1. On Products, click Add.
2. Leave AUTO on, or type a number.
3. Enter a description and optional prices.
4. Save.
5. On the next page, check the products into a location or skip.

Options and settings: Prices must be 0 or greater. Staged quantity is 1. Sample CSV headers are examples, not required names.

Expected results: Catalog rows exist. Stock exists only if check-in is saved.

Errors and troubleshooting: “Product number already exists”. “Could not verify product number”. Blank manual number shows the placeholder “No Poduct Number!”

Limitations:
- A quantity row at All Locations is inserted without a quantity column. The database default is uncertain. Do not promise a visible quantity from create alone.

Example: Add “Safety glasses” with AUTO on, then check in 10 to a shelf.
